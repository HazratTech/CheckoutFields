## 8. App Testing Information (For Shopify Review Team)

- **Test account**:  
  Check: `[x] My app doesn't require an account to use it`
  *(Shopify apps authenticate natively via store OAuth).*

- **Testing instructions**:
```text
Step-by-step instructions to test CheckoutFields:

1. Install the app onto your development store. The app authenticates automatically via Shopify OAuth.
2. Open your store's Checkout Theme Editor:
   - Navigate to Shopify Admin > Settings > Checkout.
   - Under 'Configurations', click 'Customize' on your active checkout.
3. Add the CheckoutFields block:
   - In the left sidebar under 'Checkout sections', click 'Add app block'.
   - Select 'CheckoutFields'.
   - Drag it to your desired section (e.g. after Shipping methods or under Payment).
4. Configure Field Properties:
   - In the right settings inspector, set Field Label (e.g., 'Delivery Instructions').
   - Select Field Type (Text, Multiline, Date, Checkbox, etc.).
   - Toggle 'Required Field' to test blocking validation.
   - Click 'Save' in the top right.
5. Test the Live Checkout:
   - Add any product to the cart and proceed to checkout.
   - Notice the custom CheckoutFields block rendered with native store styling.
   - Try proceeding without filling out a required field to verify blocking validation.
   - Enter values and complete the test order.
6. Verify Order Note Attributes:
   - In Shopify Admin > Orders, open the order you just placed.
   - Look at the 'Notes' or 'Additional details' section: all submitted custom field data is saved natively as order note attributes.
7. Verify App Dashboard:
   - Open 'CheckoutFields' from the Admin sidebar to review your plan status, active fields count, and documentation.
```
