How to Position Fields
Shopify Checkout is modular. You drag and place fields visually:
  1. Click "Open Checkout Editor" above.
  2. In the left sidebar, click "+ Add block".
  3. Select Fieldy Custom Checkout Fields and drag it to your desired section (Contact, Delivery, Payment).
  4. Configure your title, placeholder, and attribute key in real-time.
